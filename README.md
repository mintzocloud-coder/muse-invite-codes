## What is Muse?

**Muse is Meta's personal AI assistant** — built for the new era of AI agents. Instead of just answering questions in a chat window, Muse is designed to **do things for you**: keep working on tasks in the background after you close the app, remember context across conversations, and take actions across apps and devices.

At **Meta Connect 2026**, Meta announced a wave of new Muse capabilities:

- 🎭 A **digital avatar** you can video-chat with
- 💻 A **Mac app** that can use your computer to complete tasks
- 👓 Muse coming to Meta's **AI glasses** (hands-free via wake word)
- 🛒 Deeper **shopping partnerships and connectors**

Meta also opened an **early access program** for these features — you can ask Muse directly to put you on the list.

## ❓ FAQ

**Who made Muse?**
Meta — the company behind Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp.

**Is Muse free?**
There's a free tier with a weekly token allowance, plus paid tiers for heavier use. Invite-code tokens add on top of whatever plan you're on.

**My code didn't work. Why?**
Either that code's seats are all used up, or your 48-hour redemption window expired. Try the next code on the list.

**Where is Muse available?**
Rolling out in the US (referrals extend to Canada). You need to be 18+.

**How do I get codes to share?**
Once you're in, Muse gives you invite codes to share with others.

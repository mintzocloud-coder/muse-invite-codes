What is Muse?

Muse is Meta's personal AI assistant — built for the new era of AI agents. Instead of just answering questions in a chat window, Muse is designed to do things for you: keep working on tasks in the background after you close the app, remember context across conversations, and take actions across apps and devices.

It's made by Meta (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp) — and you can already talk to it across Meta's apps, plus the dedicated Muse app and web.

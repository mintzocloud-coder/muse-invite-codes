What is Muse?

Muse is Meta's personal AI assistant — the company's answer to the new generation of AI agents. Instead of just answering questions in a chat window, Muse is designed to do things for you: keep working on tasks in the background after you close the app, remember context across conversations, and take actions across apps and devices.

It's made by Meta (the company behind Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp), and you can talk to it across Meta's apps as well as the dedicated Muse app and web.

Why is everyone talking about it?

At its Connect 2026 developer conference, Meta announced a wave of new Muse capabilities:

  • 🎭 A digital avatar you can video-chat with
  • 🛒 Deeper shopping partnerships and connectors
  • 💻 A Mac app that can use your computer to complete tasks
  • 👓 Muse coming to Meta's AI glasses — talk to it hands-free with a wake word

Meta also opened an early access program for these new features — you can ask Muse directly to put you on the list to try them before anyone else.
